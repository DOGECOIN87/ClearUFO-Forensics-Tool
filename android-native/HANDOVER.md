## 5. What is NOT done (the honest gap list)

Nothing below is simulated in the app; each gap is stated in the UI, in `plan.md` or in `TODO.md`.

**Video**
1. Decoding of H.264, HEVC, AV1 and VP9 (the frame table is exact, the pixels are not produced).
   The app reports the codec and refuses instead of returning a wrong image.
2. Playback: no timeline, no scrubbing, no in/out points, no looping, no speed modes, no bookmarks,
   no audio, no waveform.
3. Containers not demuxed yet: WebM/Matroska, the DVR variants, the raw H.264/H.265 bitstream
   files (`src/engine/sources/dvr.ts`, `bitstream.ts`), the `Convert DVR` remuxer.
4. WebM/CodecPrivate, VP9 super-resolution and the remaining temporal paths are untested on device.

**Still images**
5. WebP and AVIF evidence are refused with a clear message (`lib/codecs/still.ts`).
6. The TIFF reader handles the common cases (uncompressed), not every TIFF flavour.
7. Lossy writers (JPEG, WebP) are not implemented; the exports are the lossless ones only.

**Viewer / measurement / presentation**
8. No zoom, no pan, no pixel readout on the viewer, no histogram/spectrum panel.
9. The annotation filters (text, shapes, spotlights, magnifiers, measurement overlays) execute but
   their geometry cannot be drawn or edited on the viewer yet: the parameter editors report the
   value and say that it is edited on the viewer in the browser workbench.
10. The measurement tools (points/lines/rects/polygons) are not implemented in the app.

**Projects / cases**
11. `.lfp` import (open a project file) and export are not implemented; cases are created and
    listed on the device only.
12. Evidence relinking after a restart (the Android document URI permission) is not implemented.
13. Bookmarks, in/out points and the undo history are not persisted in the case file.

**Reports**
14. The report is shown in the app; exporting it as a file is not implemented.

**Release**
15. No APK could be built in this environment (no Android SDK, `dl.google.com` blocked). The
    release APK, the manifest check, the device test on the Samsung Android 12 target and the
    SHA-256 of the artifact are still open.

## 6. The next milestones, in order

Do them in this order; each one ends with a commit + push.

### D — the viewer becomes a real forensic viewer
- Zoom/pan (pinch and drag) with the pixel coordinates under the finger.
- Pixel/sample readout (R, G, B, luma, 16-bit values when the source is 16-bit).
- Histogram (5 × 256: R, G, B, luma) and the spectra of the Fourier filter.
- Draw and edit the geometry of the annotation filters (the `RasterContext` in
  `lib/engine/kernels/raster.ts` already draws; it needs a coordinate layer and the parameter
  editors for `rect`, `point`, `points`, `line`, `curve`).
- Conformance: compare the drawn overlay against the browser for the shapes that do not use text;
  the text glyphs differ on purpose (own bitmap font) and must be documented as a deviation.

### E — the video engine finishes
- WebM/Matroska demuxer (EBML: Segment, Tracks, CodecPrivate, Cues, SimpleBlock/BlockGroup).
- The DVR and the bitstream paths of `src/engine/sources/{dvr,bitstream}.ts`.
- A decoder for the compressed codecs: either a pinned software decoder (large) or the platform
  decoder behind a native module, **documented as a deviation** with a comparison against the
  browser's WebCodecs output (the browser used the platform decoder too).
- The timeline: accurate frame entry, scrubbing, frame stepping, current frame/type/PTS, in/out
  range playback, looping, speed/FPS modes, bookmarks, snapshot of the current frame.
- Audio: playback, mute, volume, waveform/envelope.

### F — projects, cases and reports
- `.lfp` open/save (the schema and the migrations are already ported in `lib/shared/project.ts`),
  case metadata, bookmarks, in/out points, chain persistence.
- Evidence relinking with the Android document URI.
- The export of the case report as a file (provenance, parameters, hashes, decoder/build info).

### G — release
- Build the release APK (`./android/gradlew assembleRelease` after `expo prebuild`), inspect the
  manifest for `INTERNET`/`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, install and test on the Android 12 Samsung
  device, record the SHA-256, and update `plan.md`/`TODO.md` with the tested versions.

## 7. Rules that must not be broken

- The browser app in `src/` stays **unchanged**; it is the conformance oracle.
- Never claim "full functionality" while a subsystem is a placeholder, a metadata-only catalog, a
  playback-only approximation or an unvalidated fallback.
- Source evidence is immutable: hash the bytes as they were read, never write to the original file.
- Preserve the numeric contracts of the reference: Float32 storage points, Double intermediates,
  round-half-to-even and clamping in `toU8`, no inter-filter clamping, the LUT as a 4096-entry
  interpolated table over 0–255, the BT.601/709/2020 matrices, limited/full range, the chroma
  siting (left/center/top-left), the bicubic/bilinear/nearest chroma reconstruction, the HDR
  tone mapping, the frame order and the timestamps, the lossless writer behaviour (rgb8, rgb16 ×257,
  rgbf32 /255).
- The core stays offline: `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` stay blocked in `app.config.ts`;
  AI/MCP stays outside the core, disabled by default, explicitly network-dependent.
- Leave the unused `server/` and `drizzle/` scaffold alone; never store evidence or case data there.
- Keep the engine free of platform imports: platform code lives in `lib/project/` (that separation
  is what makes the demuxers testable in Node; `expo-file-system` cannot be imported in the vitest
  environment — it breaks the transform with `Expected 'from', got 'typeOf'`).

## 9. Traps already paid for (do not repeat them)

- **Sandbox resets wipe `node_modules/` and the global `pnpm`.** Reinstall both before running
  anything; the lockfile is committed, so `pnpm install` is enough.
- **The chain cache once served frames of a previous evidence**: the generation number of a chain
  is now a counter that never repeats (`lib/engine/graph.ts`). Keep it that way when you extend the
  engine, and keep the regression test in `tests/session.test.ts`.
- **Python string-replace patches truncated source files** during the port. Always re-read a file
  after patching it and let `pnpm check` prove it.
- ImageMagick: `gray:-` writes 16-bit (add `-depth 8`); `label:` fails on a space or a backslash.
- The MP4 sample entry starts after the version, the flags and the entry count, and its width and
  height sit 24 bytes into the visual sample entry payload.
- `expo-file-system/legacy` cannot be imported from a module that the vitest (node) environment
  loads — keep it in `lib/project/`.

The conversion is complete when the APK provides the browser app's substantive functionality
natively and offline: the filter workbench, exact still and video processing, timeline/playback/
audio, projects/cases, history/undo, measurements/annotations, provenance and reports, and the
supported import/export paths. The final handover must state: the APK path and SHA-256, the commit
and branch, the feature coverage and the known limitations, the tested Android versions, the
permission verification, the conformance and device test results, and the intentionally
unsupported formats or algorithms.
